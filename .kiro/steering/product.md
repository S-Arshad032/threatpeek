# ThreatPeek
A haunted phishing, scam message and malicious link detector (Kiroween 2026, Costume Contest).
Users paste a link, email or SMS and get a 0-100% risk score, red-flag cards, and a ghost
that reacts: Pookie (0-30), Skeptical (31-69), Poltergeist (70-100).
The haunted UI must serve the function: the ghost is the warning signal.
Never claim a message is 100% safe. Scores are estimates; say so in the UI.
